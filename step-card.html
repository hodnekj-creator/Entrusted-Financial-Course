The Stewardship Steps

Entrusted: Managing God's Money

At every step: Give first and keep a written budget. Proverbs 3:9-10; Proverbs 27:23

  1. 1Starter buffer. Save one month of essential expenses.Proverbs 21:20
  2. 2Take the match. Contribute enough to get any employer retirement match.Proverbs 13:11
  3. 3Clear consumer debt. Pay off consumer debt and keep all debt minimal.Proverbs 22:7
  4. 4Full reserve. Build 3 to 6 months of expenses.Proverbs 6:6-8
  5. 5Invest 15%. Invest 15% of income for retirement.Ecclesiastes 11:2
  6. 6Fund major goals. Save for a home, children's education and other large goals.Proverbs 24:27
  7. 7Finish and leave it better. Pay off the home, build wealth, leave an inheritance, give generously.Proverbs 13:22
