Invest shrewdly

Think ahead like the shrewd manager, and aim it at what lasts. Small amounts, given time, become large ones.

Scripture

Read each passage in your Bible and note what stands out. On the site, each reference opens in the NIV.

  • Luke 16:1-13
  • Proverbs 13:11
  • Ecclesiastes 11:2
  • Proverbs 28:20

Teaching notes

The master praises foresight, not dishonesty.

Little by little is how it grows.

Baby Step 4: invest 15%, and take the match all along.

Diversify, and distrust anything fast.

Discuss

  1. What does the shrewd manager do that you are not doing?
  2. What are you investing in that will matter in 100 years?
  3. Why is get-rich-quick so attractive? Where have you seen it go wrong?
  4. What is stopping you from starting, or from increasing, this month?

The difference

Retirement accounts cheat sheet

AccountHow it works
401(k) or 403(b)Offered through an employer. Money comes out of your paycheck automatically. Often comes with a match.
TraditionalYou skip the tax now and pay it when you take the money out in retirement.
RothYou pay the tax now, and qualified withdrawals in retirement are tax-free. Often a good fit when your income is low, as in a first job.
IRAA retirement account you open yourself, separate from any employer. Comes in traditional and Roth versions.
Index fundOne fund that owns a small piece of hundreds of companies. Built-in diversification at low cost.
Contribution limitsSet by the IRS and changed most years. Look up the current numbers before you plan.
