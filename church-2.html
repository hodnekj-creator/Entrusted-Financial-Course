8 Weeks, session 2 of 8

Work as worship

Work is the first thing you manage. You do it for the Lord, and the ability to do it is a gift.

Presenting? Page Down or the right arrow moves one section at a time.

Read these aloud from your own Bibles.

Scripture

  • Colossians 3:23-24Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.
  • Proverbs 10:4Lazy hands bring poverty; diligent hands bring wealth.
  • Deuteronomy 8:17-18Do not say your own strength produced your wealth; God gives the ability to produce it.
  • Psalm 127:2Rising early and staying up late in anxious toil is in vain; He gives His loved ones sleep.

Everyone answers.

Opening question

What is the hardest you have ever worked for something, and who were you doing it for?

Teaching point 1 of 4

Your income is your largest financial tool.

Cutting expenses has a floor. Growing what you earn through skill and diligence does not. Proverbs ties diligence to gain over and over, and a person skilled in their work stands out (Proverbs 22:29).

Teaching point 2 of 4

Who you work for changes how you work.

Paul wrote Colossians 3:23 to bondservants with no say over their jobs. The point is the audience: you work for the Lord whether or not your boss is watching, fair, or grateful.

Teaching point 3 of 4

The ability to earn is a gift, not a trophy.

Deuteronomy 8 warns against the moment success arrives and you credit yourself. Diligence is commanded and pride about the results is forbidden, both at once.

Teaching point 4 of 4

Rest is part of faithful work.

The same God who commands diligence commands Sabbath. Working every waking hour is not obedience to Colossians 3:23; it usually means the work has become the master.

Practical step

Raise your earning ability

List what you are paid for today, what skills you have that you are not yet paid for, and one skill that would raise your income in two years. Decide what it costs to get it. Then name one day a week you will rest.

The worksheet is on the handout

In the group

Discuss

  1. Would your work look different this week if the Lord were your direct supervisor?
  2. Where are you tempted to coast? Where are you tempted to overwork?
  3. Who has God used to give you the abilities you earn with?
  4. What is one skill you could build this year?

Before we meet again

This week

  • Fill in the income and skills worksheet.
  • Pick one day this week to rest, and keep it.
