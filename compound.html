The return is compounded monthly. Real returns rise and fall from year to year and are not guaranteed; try uneven years in the Year by year tab. This is a teaching tool, not investment advice.

Type a new return or monthly amount in any year. It carries into every year after, until you change it again; clear the cell to go back to the plan. A one-time amount applies to that year only; use a minus sign for a withdrawal. Total put in includes the starting balance.

What a purchase could have grown to if the same money had been invested instead.

This does not mean a purchase is wrong. It shows the full cost, so the choice is made on purpose. Returns are not guaranteed.

How this is calculated

Each amount is treated as invested on the day it would have been spent, and it grows at the annual return divided by 12 each month. Daily and weekly amounts are added up by month.
