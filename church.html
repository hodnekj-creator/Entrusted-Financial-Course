8 Weeks

Two sessions of foundation, five that walk the steps one skill at a time, and a closing session on legacy and contentment.

Who it is for
A church class or small group semester, any age.
Length
Eight 45-minute sessions

Sessions

  1. 1

    Session 1

    Whose money is it?

    God owns everything you will ever earn. Your job is to manage it faithfully, starting with what you have now.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  2. 2

    Session 2

    Work as worship

    Work is the first thing you manage. You do it for the Lord, and the ability to do it is a gift.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  3. 3

    Session 3

    Know your flocks

    A faithful manager knows exactly what he has and decides ahead of time where it goes. That is a budget.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  4. 4

    Session 4

    Give first

    Giving is the first line in the budget because it is the clearest way a manager acknowledges the owner.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  5. 5

    Session 5

    Build margin

    Wise people keep something in store. Margin turns an emergency into an inconvenience and frees you to be generous.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  6. 6

    Session 6

    Get out of debt

    The borrower serves the lender. Debt has a time and a place, but it should stay small and have an end date.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  7. 7

    Session 7

    Invest shrewdly

    Think ahead like the shrewd manager, and aim it at what lasts. Small amounts, given time, become large ones.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  8. 8

    Session 8

    Legacy and contentment

    The account outlives you. Leave an inheritance, give generously while you live, and learn the word enough.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

Print for the group

Each session has its own handout. Everyone also needs the step card, and the scripture list is handy for reading ahead. Leaders: start with how to prepare.
