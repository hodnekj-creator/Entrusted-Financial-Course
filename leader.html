Leader prep

Every session has a leader guide. Each one follows the same order: what to do before you teach, a caution to keep in mind, the timed plan, the full teaching notes, and the questions people are likely to ask.

How to prepare

  1. Read the guide's passages in your own Bible, ideally twice, a few days before you teach.
  2. Work through the handout's worksheet yourself so you can show a real example.
  3. Read the caution and the likely objections, so you are ready for the conversation the session tends to start.
  4. Print a handout for each person, plus the step card when the guide calls for it.
  5. Open the session page on the screen you will teach from, and the calculators if the session uses them.
