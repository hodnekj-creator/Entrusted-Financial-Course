Entrusted: Managing God's Money8 Weeks, session 2 of 8

Work as worship

Work is the first thing you manage. You do it for the Lord, and the ability to do it is a gift.

Scripture

Read each passage in your own Bible and note what stands out.

  • Colossians 3:23-24
  • Proverbs 10:4
  • Deuteronomy 8:17-18
  • Psalm 127:2

Teaching notes

Your income is your largest financial tool.

Who you work for changes how you work.

The ability to earn is a gift, not a trophy.

Rest is part of faithful work.

Discuss

  1. Would your work look different this week if the Lord were your direct supervisor?
  2. Where are you tempted to coast? Where are you tempted to overwork?
  3. Who has God used to give you the abilities you earn with?
  4. What is one skill you could build this year?

This week

  • Fill in the income and skills worksheet.
  • Pick one day this week to rest, and keep it.

Raise your earning ability

List what you are paid for today, what skills you have that you are not yet paid for, and one skill that would raise your income in two years. Decide what it costs to get it. Then name one day a week you will rest.
