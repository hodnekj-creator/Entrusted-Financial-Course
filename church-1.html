8 Weeks, session 1 of 8

Whose money is it?

God owns everything you will ever earn. Your job is to manage it faithfully, starting with what you have now.

Presenting? Page Down or the right arrow moves one section at a time.

Read these aloud. Tap a reference to open it in the NIV.

Scripture

  • Psalm 24:1Everything on earth belongs to the Lord.
  • 1 Chronicles 29:14David admits that what he gives to God came from God first.
  • Matthew 25:14-30The parable of the talents: a master entrusts his wealth to three servants and returns to settle accounts.

Everyone answers.

Opening question

If someone handed you $10,000 tomorrow with no strings attached, what would you do with it?

Teaching point 1 of 4

God owns it. You manage it.

Psalm 24:1 leaves nothing out. A manager makes real decisions and has real freedom, but he never forgets whose name is on the account. Picture house-sitting: you live there and make choices every day, and you still do not repaint the kitchen. The $10,000 always has strings attached.

Teaching point 2 of 4

The manager's job is faithfulness, not size.

The servant with two bags hears the same words as the servant with five. Two people on very different incomes are measured the same way: what did you do with what you were handed? The parable does not promise that faithful people get rich. It promises that the master notices.

Teaching point 3 of 4

A wrong view of the owner produces fear.

The third servant buried the money because he saw the master as harsh. How you picture God shapes how you handle money: fear hides and hoards, trust puts it to work.

Teaching point 4 of 4

Giving shows who you think the owner is.

The tithe is Old Testament law and a good practice to learn from. The New Testament instruction in 2 Corinthians 9:7 is to give what you have decided in your heart, cheerfully. Decide on purpose and pray over the number. If giving has never stretched you, and you have never given a tenth, pray over it more.

Practical step

Take inventory

A manager cannot be faithful with what he has not counted. Fill in the inventory: what you hold, what you owe, what comes in. Then read the step card once from top to bottom. The rest of the course walks through it.

The worksheet is on the handout

In the group

Discuss

  1. Where do you most naturally think "this is mine": your paycheck, your time, your stuff?
  2. The third servant acted out of fear. Where does fear drive your money decisions?
  3. What would change this week if you were managing your money for someone you had to report to?
  4. What is one thing you are already managing well?

Before we meet again

This week

  • Fill in the "What I manage" inventory.
  • Decide what you will give from your next paycheck, and pray over the number before you settle it.
