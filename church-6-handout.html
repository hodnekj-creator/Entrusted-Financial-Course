Entrusted: Managing God's Money8 Weeks, session 6 of 8

Get out of debt

The borrower serves the lender. Debt has a time and a place, but it should stay small and have an end date.

Scripture

Read each passage in your own Bible and note what stands out.

  • Proverbs 22:7
  • Romans 13:8
  • Proverbs 22:26-27
  • Psalm 37:21

Teaching notes

Scripture warns about debt without forbidding it.

Keep it minimal, and repay what you owe.

Credit cards: paid in full, or not at all.

Pick a payoff order and attack.

Discuss

  1. How does owing money affect your freedom to give, change jobs, or say yes to God?
  2. Which would keep you going: quick wins or the lowest total cost?
  3. If you use a credit card, do you spend differently than you would with cash?
  4. Has anyone asked you to co-sign? What does Proverbs 22:26-27 say to that?

This week

  • Fill in the debt payoff plan and pick your order.
  • Find one expense to cut and send that amount to the first debt this month.

Build your payoff plan

List every debt with its balance, APR and minimum payment. Number them in snowball order and in avalanche order, then choose one. Pay minimums on everything and all extra on the first. When it is gone, roll its payment onto the next.

Debt payoff plan

Snowball: smallest balance first, for momentum. Avalanche: highest APR first, for the lowest total cost. Pay minimums on all, and put every extra dollar on the first one in your order.
