Know your flocks

A faithful manager knows exactly what he has and decides ahead of time where it goes. That is a budget.

Scripture

Read each passage in your Bible and note what stands out. On the site, each reference opens in the NIV.

  • Proverbs 27:23-24
  • Luke 14:28-30
  • Proverbs 21:5

Teaching notes

A budget is knowing your flocks.

Decide before the month starts.

Every dollar gets a job.

The budget serves you.

Discuss

  1. What comes to mind when you hear the word budget: freedom or restriction? Why?
  2. Which category do you least want to look at?
  3. If you are married, who knows the numbers? Is that working?
  4. What would you do with money you found by tracking?

This week

  • Write a budget for next month before it starts.
  • Track every dollar for 30 days.

Write next month's budget

Start with income after tax. Write giving first, then saving, then needs, then wants, until the remainder is zero. Track actual spending for one month in whatever tool you will really use: an app, a spreadsheet or an envelope of cash.

Monthly budget

Give every dollar a job before the month starts. Giving and saving go first, not last.
