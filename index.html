Entrusted: Managing God's Money

God owns it all, we manage it, and He will ask for an account.

Psalm 24:1; Matthew 25:14-30

Entrusted is a Bible-based course on handling money as a manager of what belongs to God. It is written for church classes and small groups, and it follows The Stewardship Steps: seven steps from a one-month starter buffer to leaving an inheritance, with giving and a written budget at every step. Every session has a page to present, a printable handout and a leader guide.

Three ways to teach it

One Night

One 90-minute session

A church small group that wants the whole picture in a single evening.

About 40 minutes of teaching and 50 of reading and conversation. Covers the spine and the step card, and points to the longer tracks for the how-to.

See the session

4 Weeks

Four 45-minute sessions

College students close to graduation and people in their first few years of work.

Keep living like a student, understand loans and rates before you sign, and start investing while time is on your side.

See the sessions

8 Weeks

Eight 45-minute sessions

A church class or small group semester, any age.

Two sessions of foundation, five that walk the steps one skill at a time, and a closing session on legacy and contentment.

See the sessions

The Stewardship Steps

At every step: Give first and keep a written budget.

Read each step with its scripture

Print the step card

  1. 1

    Step 1: Starter buffer

    Save one month of essential expenses.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Take the match

    Contribute enough to get any employer retirement match.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Clear consumer debt

    Pay off consumer debt and keep all debt minimal.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Full reserve

    Build 3 to 6 months of expenses.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Invest 15%

    Invest 15% of income for retirement.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Fund major goals

    Save for a home, children's education and other large goals.

  7. 7

    Step 7: Finish and leave it better

    Pay off the home, build wealth, leave an inheritance, give generously.
