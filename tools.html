Tools

Two calculators used in the sessions. They run entirely in your browser: nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

For a growth chart, a year-by-year spreadsheet you can edit, and an opportunity cost calculator, open the compound interest page.

Compound growth

What a steady monthly amount could grow to by a given age, and what starting ten years later costs.

The return is an assumption, compounded monthly. Real markets rise and fall, and returns are not guaranteed. This is a teaching tool, not investment advice.

How this is calculated

Ending value = P × ((1 + r/12)n − 1) ÷ (r/12), where P is the monthly amount, r is the annual return as a decimal, and n is the number of months between the two ages. Each deposit is made at the end of the month.

Debt payoff

How long a balance takes to pay off at a fixed monthly payment, and what it costs in interest.

Assumes the APR stays the same and nothing new is charged. Interest is figured monthly at the APR divided by 12.
