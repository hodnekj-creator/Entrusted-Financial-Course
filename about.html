About this course

God owns it all, we manage it, and He will ask for an account.

What Entrusted is

Entrusted: Managing God's Money is a Bible-based course on handling money as a manager of what belongs to God. It is written for church classes, small groups and campus ministry groups, and it comes in three formats: One Night, a single 90-minute session; 4 Weeks, for students near graduation and people in their first years of work; and 8 Weeks, for a church class or small group of any age.

The course follows The Stewardship Steps, seven steps taken in order, with giving and a written budget at every step.

Teaching, not financial advice

This is teaching for churches and small groups. It is not professional financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not recommend specific investments, funds, banks, apps or advisors. Contribution limits, interest rates and tax rules change, so look up the current figures before you plan, and talk with a qualified professional about your own situation.

Investment examples assume a 7% annual return to show how time and steady saving work. Returns are not guaranteed.

Scripture

Passages are given as references with a short paraphrase, not quoted in full. Bring your own Bible and read each passage there. The scripture list collects every reference in the course.

Privacy

This site has no sign-ups, no tracking and no outside scripts. The calculators run in your browser and nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.
