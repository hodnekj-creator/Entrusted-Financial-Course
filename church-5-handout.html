Entrusted: Managing God's Money8 Weeks, session 5 of 8

Build margin

Wise people keep something in store. Margin turns an emergency into an inconvenience and frees you to be generous.

Scripture

Read each passage in your own Bible and note what stands out.

  • Proverbs 21:20
  • Proverbs 6:6-8
  • Genesis 41:34-36
  • Matthew 6:25-34

Teaching notes

Emergencies are certain. Only the date is unknown.

Step 1: one month of essentials.

Step 4: three to six months.

Margin is not hoarding.

Discuss

  1. Would a $1,500 surprise this month go on a card, come from savings, or come from family?
  2. Where is the line between wise saving and hoarding?
  3. How does having margin change your ability to give when a need appears?
  4. What could you cut or sell to fill the starter buffer faster?

This week

  • Calculate your one-month number.
  • Open a separate savings account and schedule the first transfer.

Size your emergency fund

Add up one month of essentials. That is your starter buffer. Multiply by three and six for the full reserve. Put it in a separate savings account, and set an automatic transfer on payday.

Emergency fund

Keep it in a separate savings account so it is easy to reach and hard to spend by accident.
