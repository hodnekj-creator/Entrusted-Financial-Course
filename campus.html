4 Weeks

Keep living like a student, understand loans and rates before you sign, and start investing while time is on your side.

Who it is for
College students close to graduation and people in their first few years of work.
Length
Four 45-minute sessions

Sessions

  1. 1

    Session 1

    Whose money is it?

    God owns everything you will ever earn. Your job is to manage it faithfully, starting with the small amounts you have now.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  2. 2

    Session 2

    Keep living like a student

    When the first real paycheck arrives, keep spending like you did in college. The gap is your one-time head start.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  3. 3

    Session 3

    Debt and the fine print

    Borrowing puts someone else in charge of part of your future paychecks. Understand exactly what you are signing, and keep it small.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

  4. 4

    Session 4

    Small things, long time

    Little things now make a big difference later. Time is the one advantage you have that older people cannot buy.

    PresentHandoutLeader guide

Print for the group

Each session has its own handout. Everyone also needs the step card, and the scripture list is handy for reading ahead. Leaders: start with how to prepare.
