Entrusted: Managing God's Money8 Weeks, session 4 of 8

Give first

Giving is the first line in the budget because it is the clearest way a manager acknowledges the owner.

Scripture

Read each passage in your own Bible and note what stands out.

  • Proverbs 3:9-10
  • 2 Corinthians 9:6-7
  • Malachi 3:10
  • Mark 12:41-44

Teaching notes

First, not leftover.

The tithe is a starting point to learn from, not a law over you.

Decide it, and pray over it.

God measures the heart and the proportion.

Discuss

  1. Has giving ever cost you something you felt? What happened?
  2. Is your giving decided, or does it depend on how the month went?
  3. What makes giving feel reluctant for you?
  4. How do you hold together giving first and getting out of debt?

This week

  • Fill in the giving plan.
  • Pray over the number every day this week before you settle it.

Make a giving plan

Write what you gave last month and what share of income it was. Decide a number for next month, where it goes, and the date it leaves your account. Automate it if you can, and review it whenever your income changes.
