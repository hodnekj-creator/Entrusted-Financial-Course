Entrusted: Managing God's Money4 Weeks, session 2 of 4

Keep living like a student

When the first real paycheck arrives, keep spending like you did in college. The gap is your one-time head start.

Scripture

Read each passage in your own Bible and note what stands out.

  • Proverbs 21:20
  • Proverbs 21:5
  • 1 Timothy 6:6-8
  • Luke 12:15

Teaching notes

You already know how to live on little.

Lifestyle creep is the default.

Contentment is learned early or expensively.

Write the budget before the paycheck.

Discuss

  1. What part of student life would you be glad to keep for two more years? What part would be hard?
  2. Whose lifestyle are you tempted to match after graduation?
  3. What does enough look like for you at 25?
  4. What would you do with an extra $1,000 a month for two years?

This week

  • Fill in the first-paycheck budget.
  • Add up one month of essentials: that is your starter buffer target.

Write your first-paycheck budget

Fill in what you spend now in each category. Then fill in the salary column, keeping every spending line as close to today as you can. Whatever is left goes to giving, the starter buffer and loans.
