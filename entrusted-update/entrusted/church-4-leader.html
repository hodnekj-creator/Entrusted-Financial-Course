8 Weeks, session 4 of 8

Leader guide: Give first

Giving is the first line in the budget because it is the clearest way a manager acknowledges the owner.

Session pageHandout

Before you teach

  • Talk with your pastor about how your church teaches giving.
  • Be ready to share, humbly, a time giving stretched you.
  • Print handouts.

Caution

Do not teach Malachi 3:10 as a formula where giving guarantees financial return. Do not use guilt. The position here: Old Testament law, good biblical practice, give what you have decided in your heart, and pray, especially if you have never been stretched.

Teaching notes

Big idea

Giving is the first line in the budget because it is the clearest way a manager acknowledges the owner.

Scripture

Proverbs 3:9-10
Honor the Lord with your wealth and with the first of what you produce.
2 Corinthians 9:6-7
Each should give what they have decided in their heart, not reluctantly or under pressure, for God loves a cheerful giver.
Malachi 3:10
Israel is told to bring the whole tithe into the storehouse.
Mark 12:41-44
A widow's two small coins are counted as more than the large gifts of the rich.

Opening question

What is the most generous thing anyone has ever done for you?

Teaching points

  1. First, not leftover.

    Proverbs 3:9 speaks of the first of the harvest. Giving from what is left at the end of the month says the owner gets whatever the manager did not want.

  2. The tithe is a starting point to learn from, not a law over you.

    A tenth was Old Testament law for Israel, and Malachi 3 speaks into that covenant. It is a good and tested practice. The New Testament instruction is to give what you have decided in your heart.

  3. Decide it, and pray over it.

    Decided means on purpose, not by impulse or guilt. If giving has never stretched you, and you have never given a tenth, that is a reason to pray over it more, not a reason to feel safe.

  4. God measures the heart and the proportion.

    The widow gave almost nothing in amount and everything in proportion. The Macedonians gave out of poverty (2 Corinthians 8:1-5). Generosity is not reserved for step 6.

Practical step: Make a giving plan

Write what you gave last month and what share of income it was. Decide a number for next month, where it goes, and the date it leaves your account. Automate it if you can, and review it whenever your income changes.

Discussion questions

  1. Has giving ever cost you something you felt? What happened?
  2. Is your giving decided, or does it depend on how the month went?
  3. What makes giving feel reluctant for you?
  4. How do you hold together giving first and getting out of debt?

This week's action

  • Fill in the giving plan.
  • Pray over the number every day this week before you settle it.

Likely objections

Should I give while I am in debt?
Yes, in this course giving comes first at every step. The amount is between you and God; the habit should not wait.
Gross or net?
Scripture does not say. Decide in your heart and be consistent. Do not let the question delay starting.
Does it all have to go to the church?
Talk with your pastor. Supporting your local church first and giving beyond it is a common pattern.
