Entrusted: Managing God's Money

God owns it all, we manage it, and He will ask for an account.

Psalm 24:1; Matthew 25:14-30

Entrusted is a Bible-based course on handling money as a manager of what belongs to God. It is written for church classes and small groups, and it follows Dave Ramsey's Baby Steps, from a $1,000 starter emergency fund to building wealth and giving, with scripture for each step and giving and a written budget at every one. Every session has a page to present, a printable handout and a leader guide.

Three ways to teach it

One Night

One 90-minute session

A church small group that wants the whole picture in a single evening.

The whole picture in one evening: whose money it is, the Baby Steps, and a practical section on compound interest, APR and APY, how tax brackets work, the 401(k) match and the stock market. Points to the longer tracks for the how-to.

See the session

4 Weeks

Four 45-minute sessions

College students close to graduation and people in their first few years of work.

Keep living like a student, understand loans and rates before you sign, and start investing while time is on your side.

See the sessions

8 Weeks

Eight 45-minute sessions

A church class or small group semester, any age.

Two sessions of foundation, five that walk the steps one skill at a time, and a closing session on legacy and contentment.

The Baby Steps

The Baby Steps are Dave Ramsey's plan, taught in his books and in Financial Peace University. This course follows them, without his college-fund step, and adds scripture to each step. Because of that, our step 5 and step 6 are his step 6 and step 7. Baby Steps 1 to 3 are done one at a time, in order. Baby Steps 4 and 5 are worked at the same time.

At every step: Give first and keep a written budget.
