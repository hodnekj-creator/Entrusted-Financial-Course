Money basics

Compound interest
Growth on growth. Ten early years can beat thirty-three later ones.
Rule of 72
72 divided by the return is the years to double. At 7%, about 10.
Debt at 24%
$5,000 at 24% with $100 a month is never paid off. At $200, 36 months.
APR and APY
APR is the rate you pay on loans. APY is what you earn on savings.
Taxes and brackets
Each rate applies only to its slice of income. A raise never lowers take-home.
401(k) match
Get the full employer match. It is part of your pay.
Stocks and index funds
A share is a piece of a company. An index fund owns pieces of hundreds.

More on each, with charts and articles: hodnekj-creator.github.io/Entrusted-Financial-Course/basics.html
