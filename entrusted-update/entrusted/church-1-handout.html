Entrusted: Managing God's Money8 Weeks, session 1 of 8

Whose money is it?

God owns everything you will ever earn. Your job is to manage it faithfully, starting with what you have now.

Scripture

Read each passage in your Bible and note what stands out. On the site, each reference opens in the NIV.

  • Psalm 24:1
  • 1 Chronicles 29:14
  • Matthew 25:14-30

Teaching notes

God owns it. You manage it.

The manager's job is faithfulness, not size.

A wrong view of the owner produces fear.

Giving shows who you think the owner is.

Discuss

  1. Where do you most naturally think "this is mine": your paycheck, your time, your stuff?
  2. The third servant acted out of fear. Where does fear drive your money decisions?
  3. What would change this week if you were managing your money for someone you had to report to?
  4. What is one thing you are already managing well?

This week

  • Fill in the "What I manage" inventory.
  • Decide what you will give from your next paycheck, and pray over the number before you settle it.

Take inventory

A manager cannot be faithful with what he has not counted. Fill in the inventory: what you hold, what you owe, what comes in. Then read the step card once from top to bottom. The rest of the course walks through it.
