Work with all your heart, as working for the Lord.

Session 2: Keep living like a student

Proverbs 21:20
The wise store up; fools consume all they have.
Proverbs 21:5
The plans of the diligent lead to profit; haste leads to poverty.
1 Timothy 6:6-8
Godliness with contentment is great gain; with food and clothing we will be content.

Life does not consist in an abundance of possessions.

Do not put up security for another's debts.

Proverbs 13:11

8 Weeks

Session 1: Whose money is it?

Psalm 24:1
Everything on earth belongs to the Lord.
1 Chronicles 29:14
David admits that what he gives to God came from God first.
Matthew 25:14-30
The parable of the talents: a master entrusts his wealth to three servants and returns to settle accounts.

Also mentioned: 2 Corinthians 9:7; Deuteronomy 8:17-18; Luke 16:10; Proverbs 30:8-9

Session 2: Work as worship

Colossians 3:23-24
Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.
Proverbs 10:4
Lazy hands bring poverty; diligent hands bring wealth.
Deuteronomy 8:17-18
Do not say your own strength produced your wealth; God gives the ability to produce it.
Psalm 127:2
Rising early and staying up late in anxious toil is in vain; He gives His loved ones sleep.

Also mentioned: Exodus 20:8-10; Proverbs 22:29; 1 Timothy 6:9-10

Session 3: Know your flocks

Proverbs 27:23-24
Know the condition of your flocks and give careful attention to your herds, because riches do not last forever.
Luke 14:28-30
No one builds a tower without first sitting down to estimate the cost.
Proverbs 21:5
The plans of the diligent lead to profit; haste leads to poverty.

Session 4: Give first

Proverbs 3:9-10
Honor the Lord with your wealth and with the first of what you produce.
2 Corinthians 9:6-7
Each should give what they have decided in their heart, not reluctantly or under pressure, for God loves a cheerful giver.
Malachi 3:10
Israel is told to bring the whole tithe into the storehouse.
Mark 12:41-44
A widow's two small coins are counted as more than the large gifts of the rich.

Also mentioned: 2 Corinthians 8:1-5

Session 5: Build margin

Proverbs 21:20
The wise store up choice food and oil; fools consume all they have.
Proverbs 6:6-8
The ant stores provisions in summer and gathers at harvest.
Genesis 41:34-36
Joseph stores grain in seven good years to carry Egypt through seven lean ones.
Matthew 6:25-34
Do not worry about tomorrow; your Father knows what you need.

Also mentioned: Luke 12

Session 6: Get out of debt

Proverbs 22:7
The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is slave to the lender.
Romans 13:8
Let no debt remain outstanding except the continuing debt to love one another.
Proverbs 22:26-27
Do not put up security for debts; if you cannot pay, your bed will be taken from under you.
Psalm 37:21
The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the righteous give generously.

Session 7: Invest shrewdly

Luke 16:1-13
A manager about to lose his job uses his last days to secure his future, and the master commends his shrewdness.
Proverbs 13:11
Dishonest money dwindles, but whoever gathers little by little makes it grow.
Ecclesiastes 11:2
Invest in seven ventures, even eight; you do not know what disaster may come.
Proverbs 28:20
A faithful person will be richly blessed, but one eager to get rich will not go unpunished.

Session 8: Legacy and contentment

Proverbs 13:22
A good person leaves an inheritance for their children's children.
1 Timothy 6:6-10
Godliness with contentment is great gain; the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil.
1 Timothy 6:17-19
The rich are told not to hope in wealth but to be rich in good deeds, generous and willing to share.
Luke 12:13-21
The rich fool builds bigger barns for himself and is not rich toward God.

Also mentioned: Proverbs 30:8-9; Proverbs 20:21; Philippians 4:11-13
