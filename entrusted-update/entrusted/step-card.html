The Baby Steps

From Dave Ramsey · Entrusted: Managing God's Money

At every step: Give first and keep a written budget. Proverbs 3:9-10; Proverbs 27:23

  1. 1Save $1,000 for your starter emergency fund.Proverbs 21:20
  2. 2Pay off all debt (except the house) using the debt snowball.Proverbs 22:7
  3. 3Save 3–6 months of expenses in a fully funded emergency fund.Proverbs 6:6-8
  4. 4Invest 15% of your household income in retirement.Ecclesiastes 11:2
  5. 5Pay off your home early.Romans 13:8
  6. 6Build wealth and give.Proverbs 13:22

Steps 4 and 5 are worked together. Course notes: take any employer match at every step. If you keep a credit card, pay it in full every month.
